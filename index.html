AI & Research

ProgFlex Tutor

AI-Enhanced Adaptive Coding Tutor for Beginner Programmers

ProgFlex Tutor is my final-year capstone research platform built at the University of Colombo to support undergraduate students learning C programming. The system pairs Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) with a Q-learning reinforcement learning agent to dynamically select question difficulty and sequence learning interventions based on each student’s evolving mastery and misconception patterns.

  • — Designed and built the full-stack learning platform, C programming question bank, automated evaluation engine, and adaptive sequencing architecture.
  • — Applied Bayesian Knowledge Tracing (BKT) to continuously estimate concept mastery and surface recurring beginner C misconceptions.
  • — Trained and evaluated a Q-learning policy to adaptively sequence problem difficulty and personalised interventions per learner state.
  • — Completed empirical evaluation and defended the system as the final-year BICT (Honours) research capstone.
